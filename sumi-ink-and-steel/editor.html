Level → Rooms

A level is a map of connected rooms. Each room is a box. The box size becomes the playable room size. Open a box to place backgrounds, depth lanes, paths, enemies, NPCs, objects, puzzles, and exits.

Room-based level design flow

1
Make the level map. Click Add room box. Each box is one playable room; its width/height are the room's playable size.
2
Connect rooms. Use Connect Rooms, or open a room and place an Exit/Door. The game shows an E prompt at those markers.
3
Open a room. Add a background, then draw the walkable path on the floor. Depth lanes show far/back, middle, and near/front so you can place objects at the right visual depth.
4
Add gameplay. Place enemies, NPCs, keys, switches, and puzzle objects. Save to game files, then playtest the selected room.

Navigation: mouse wheel zooms. Right-drag, Space+drag, or Pan tool moves the view. Delete removes selected things.
