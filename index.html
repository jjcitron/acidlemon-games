acidlemon games

Two browser games built as proof of concepts. Source is in this repo; the links below are the live, full-audio versions.

Sumi: Ink and Steel

2D samurai brawler in a late-Edo bloody-woodblock style. Sprites generated by a local ComfyUI + Flux pipeline. Includes a level editor.
Play ▸ samurai-action-game.vercel.app  ·  Source ▸ ./sumi-ink-and-steel/

3D Harrier

Head-tracked rail shooter, a Space Harrier homage. The webcam re-projects the scene so your monitor becomes a window. Three.js, original geometry and audio.
