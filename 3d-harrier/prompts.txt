3D HARRIER — IMAGE + MUSIC GENERATION PROMPTS (Levels 9-18)
===========================================================

Workflow:
- IMAGE PROMPTS: paste into Gemini (or any AI image generator). Aspect ratio 16:9, vertical-tall framing if your splash is portrait. Save the result as `sound/<filename>.png`.
- MUSIC PROMPTS: paste into Suno / Udio / Gemini's music generation. Target length ~70-90 seconds, loopable. Save as `sound/<filename>.mp3`.
- After generating, update `src/game/content/stages.js` STAGE_MEDIA to point to the new files, or use the editor's SPLASH IMAGE upload button for custom levels.

Style anchor across all levels:
- "1985 Space Harrier-inspired retro arcade splash"
- "saturated retro neon, dramatic horizon, low-poly silhouette aesthetic"
- "cinematic widescreen composition, art direction by Yu Suzuki"
- Music: "synth-driven retro arcade, FM-bass, fast BPM 140-160, looping, energetic, no vocals"
