3D HARRIER - SUNO MUSIC QUEUE (Levels 9-24)
============================================

Paste each block into Suno's "Custom Mode" prompt. Set:
- Mode: Instrumental (no vocals)
- Style: matches the genre/BPM cues in each block
- Length: tracks loop, so generate ~80-130 sec depending on level

After generation, download as MP3 and save to:
  C:\Dev\Personal\3d screen\sound\<filename>.mp3

Then update STAGE_MEDIA in src/game/content/stages.js to point each
stage at its new file.
